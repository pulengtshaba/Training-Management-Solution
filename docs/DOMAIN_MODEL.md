# DOMAIN_MODEL.md

# Training Management System

## Domain Model Document

Version: 1.0

Architecture: Clean Architecture (Monolithic)

Domain Approach: Domain-Driven Design (DDD Concepts)

---

# 1. Purpose

This document describes the core business concepts of the Training Management System (TMS).

Rather than focusing on database tables or API endpoints, it defines the business entities, their responsibilities, and the business rules that govern how they interact.

---

# 2. Domain Overview

The Training Management System manages the complete lifecycle of employee training, including:

* Employee management
* Training programmes
* Training sessions
* Registrations
* Attendance
* Notifications
* Reporting

The domain model ensures that business rules are enforced consistently regardless of how the application is accessed.

---

# 3. Ubiquitous Language

The following terms are used consistently across the business and the codebase:

| Business Term      | Meaning                                                    |
| ------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| Employee           | A person employed by the organisation.                     |
| Trainer            | A user responsible for delivering training sessions.       |
| Manager            | A user responsible for managing employees within a branch. |
| Administrator      | A user with full system privileges.                        |
| Branch             | A physical business location.                              |
| Venue              | A location where training is conducted.                    |
| Training Programme | A reusable training course definition.                     |
| Training Session   | A scheduled occurrence of a programme.                     |
| Registration       | An employee's enrolment in a training session.             |
| Attendance         | The participation record for a registration.               |
| Notification       | A message generated by the system.                         |

# 4. Domain Entities

## Employee

### Description

Represents an individual who can participate in training.

### Responsibilities

* Maintain personal information.
* Register for training.
* View training history.
* Receive notifications.

### Business Rules

* Employee number must be unique.
* Email address must be unique.
* Employee belongs to exactly one branch.
* Employee has exactly one role.

---

## Branch

Represents an organisational location.

Responsibilities:

* Own employees.
* Own venues.

Business Rules:

* Branch name must be unique.
* Branch code must be unique.

---

## Training Programme

Represents a reusable training offering.

Responsibilities:

* Define course content.
* Define expected duration.
* Define programme availability.

Business Rules:

* Programme title must be unique.
* Inactive programmes cannot accept new sessions.

---

## Training Session

Represents a scheduled delivery of a programme.

Responsibilities:

* Schedule dates.
* Assign trainer.
* Assign venue.
* Track participant capacity.

Business Rules:

* End date must be after start date.
* Capacity must be greater than zero.
* Session must reference an active programme.
* Session must reference an available venue.

---

## Registration

Represents an employee's enrolment in a training session.

Responsibilities:

* Link employee to session.
* Track registration status.

Business Rules:

* An employee may register only once for the same session.
* Registration is not allowed if the session is full.
* Registration is not allowed after the session has started (unless explicitly permitted by business policy).

---

## Attendance

Represents attendance for a registration.

Responsibilities:

* Record attendance outcome.
* Record check-in and check-out times.

Business Rules:

* Attendance cannot exist without a valid registration.
* Attendance may only be recorded for scheduled sessions.
* Attendance status must be one of:

  * Pending
  * Attended
  * Missed
  * Cancelled

---

## Notification

Represents a system-generated message.

Responsibilities:

* Notify users of important events.
* Track delivery status.
* Track read status.

Business Rules:

* A notification may have multiple recipients.
* A recipient can mark a notification as read.

# 6. Aggregates

## Training Session Aggregate

Aggregate Root:

Training Session

Contains:

* Registrations
* Attendance

Responsibilities:

* Enforce participant capacity.
* Prevent duplicate registrations.
* Validate attendance rules.

---

## Employee Aggregate

Aggregate Root:

Employee

Contains:

* Registrations
* Notifications

Responsibilities:

* Manage employee training history.
* Manage notification preferences.

---

# 9. Business Invariants

The following rules must always be true:

* Every employee belongs to one branch.
* Every training session belongs to one programme.
* Every registration references one employee and one training session.
* A session cannot exceed its maximum capacity.
* Duplicate registrations are not permitted.
* Attendance requires a valid registration.

These invariants are enforced within the domain model.

---

# 10. Bounded Context

The current application is implemented as a modular monolith with a single bounded context:

**Training Management**

Future versions may separate responsibilities into additional bounded contexts such as:

* Identity & Access Management
* Learning Management
* Reporting & Analytics
* Notifications

---

# 11. Domain Model Summary

The domain model is the heart of the application.

It ensures that business rules remain independent of:

* Database implementation
* User interface
* Framework-specific code
* External integrations

By modelling the business concepts directly, the system remains easier to understand, maintain, and extend as requirements evolve.
